For NewGames.ai questions, corrections, or general inquiries, use the contact address that best fits your message.

- General contact: [hello@newgames.ai](mailto:hello@newgames.ai)
- Support: [support@newgames.ai](mailto:support@newgames.ai)
- Game submissions / partnerships: [games@newgames.ai](mailto:games@newgames.ai)
- Privacy inquiries: [privacy@newgames.ai](mailto:privacy@newgames.ai)

Please include enough context for the team to understand the request. If your message is about a specific page, include the page URL and the detail you want reviewed.

Contact emails are used only for responding to inquiries.

## Guide Feedback

Was a step unclear, or did you need an example we did not include? Send your question to [hello@newgames.ai](mailto:hello@newgames.ai). Include the guide's URL and the point where you got stuck. A short description is enough; you do not need to share your scores or play history.

For a correction, identify the sentence or link and explain what seems wrong. If an official source helps explain the issue, include its URL. Please do not send passwords, account details, browser files, or other private information.

Questions about a game's missing progress or controls belong with that game's official help or support. NewGames.ai can review its own guides, but cannot recover saves or change another site's game.

We cannot promise a reply time or a new article for every request. You can find published guides and editorial corrections on [New & Updated](/updates/).
