## Starting The Daily Puzzle {#the-key9-first-flow}

The current game starts with the daily Sudoku board. Use the given numbers to work out what belongs in each empty cell. Each row, column, and 3-by-3 box needs the digits 1 through 9 without repetition.

Your first goal can be one cell rather than the entire board: choose a space where the surrounding clues narrow the possibilities. The official [How to Play guide](https://daysudoku.com/how-to-play/) explains the current rules and controls.
